# 3-Minute Demo Script

Before recording: select **Reset Demo** (sidebar), then open the Overview.

| Time | Screen | Action | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Overview | — | "Modern products contain thousands of interconnected parts. When one supplier changes a component, understanding the impact across products, cost, margin, and teams can take days." |
| 0:20–0:45 | New Change Event | **Analyze a Change → Use Demo Notice**. Gemini extracts MCU-X100, End-of-Life, last time buy Jan 30, 2027, replacement MCU-X200. Select **Analyze Impact**. | "RippleOps reads the supplier notice and validates the part against our product data." |
| 0:45–1:20 | Impact Analysis | Graph appears. Point at KPIs. | "12 affected SKUs across 3 product families, $4.2M of revenue exposure, and only 58 days of inventory coverage." |
| 1:20–1:50 | Impact Analysis | Click **P300** in the graph. Show both paths and the "Why this matters" explanation. | "P300 uses 4 MCU-X100 units through two BOM paths and runs close to the 20% target margin." |
| 1:50–2:15 | Scenario Simulator | **Run Scenario** with MCU-X200 (or Quick scenario "Replacement only"). | "MCU-X200 costs 18% more: +$4.73 per product and −1.9 points of margin. 3 products drop below target." |
| 2:15–2:35 | Scenario Simulator | Set Copper +12% and TW → US Ocean +15%, **Run Scenario**. | "Add copper and freight, and 5 products fall below 20% gross margin." |
| 2:35–2:55 | Recommended Response | Select **Recommended Response**. Highlight Procurement last-time-buy, Engineering validation, Finance pricing review, Sales quote review. | "Every action cites the calculated facts behind it." |
| 2:55–3:00 | — | End card. | "One change. Every impact. One decision." |

Optional: open **Ask RippleOps** and ask "Why is P300 high risk?" or
"What happens if copper increases by 12%?" to show grounded, tool-backed answers.
